As determined by the Reitan test that established level of hepatic encephalopathy, The best therapeutic effect was found in patients who received JETEPAR

Conclusion: The drug used in group I possessed increased antihypoxant properties but smaller hepatoprotective properties than the drug used in group II. When signs of toxic hepatitis are predominant, it would be more preferable to used the drug applied in group II ( JETEPAR ) and when the signs of tissue hypoxia and predominent the drug applied in group I shoud be used.
